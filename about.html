Greetings from Phee

Hi, I’m Giuseppe—and this is Phee, who’s here to say hello (and occasionally steal the spotlight).

So, what’s Phee Playground?

It’s my personal project and a bit of a portfolio: a place to try ideas, learn by making, and collect things that are fun to click around.

I built it with AI-assisted development—sometimes called vibe coding. I bring the ideas and steer the project; AI helps me turn them into working code. It’s been a fun way to experiment, learn, and make something of my own.
